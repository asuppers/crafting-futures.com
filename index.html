Who We Are

A small team with a big heart

Crafting Futures is a volunteer initiative founded by a group of passionate Austrians who believe that creativity and practical skills can transform lives. In partnership with Hands for an African Child (HFAAC) in Bukomero, Uganda, we run hands-on crafting and skills programs for children, teachers, and mothers.

Our first project took place in November 2025 at NURI Primary School in Bukomero — and we are already back in July 2026 with new workshops, new ideas and even more energy.

We are not a large NGO. We are people who show up, roll up their sleeves and craft something meaningful — together with the community.

Our Projects

From idea to reality

Each visit brings new workshops, new skills and new friendships. Here is what we have done so far.

📅 October 31 – November 13, 2025 · NURI Primary School, Bukomero

Project 1 – Volunteer Skills Program

Our first two-week stay introduced practical crafting and technical skills to children, teachers and parents. Through interactive workshops we taught woodworking, crocheting, sewing and much more — leaving behind three trained local craft teachers to continue the program.

🪵 Wood Crafting 🔑 Wooden Keychains ✂️ Cutting Boards 🧶 Crochet Workshops 🪡 Hand Sewing 🧸 Handmade Dolls 🪢 Door Mats 👑 Felt Crowns 👩‍🏫 Teacher Training 👩 Mothers Training
📅 July 2026 · NURI Primary School, Bukomero

Project 2 – Building on What We Started

Back in Bukomero with new energy and new workshops! Building on the foundation of our first visit, we expanded the program with advanced skills, more participants and a growing vision for a self-sustaining craft education centre.

🪵 Advanced Woodworking 🧶 Advanced Crochet 🪡 Sewing ♻️ Creative Recycling 👩‍🏫 Teacher Development

Future Vision

Where we are going

With continued support, Crafting Futures aims to grow into a self-sustaining craft education centre that empowers generations of young people in Bukomero.

1

Expand the teaching team

Employ one additional male craft teacher to hold weekly workshops alongside the existing two female teachers.

2

Regular volunteer visits

Yearly or semi-annual visits to introduce new projects, provide advanced training and strengthen community bonds.

3

New courses

Develop sewing, basic carpentry, creative recycling and a Creative Technology Workshop with 3D printing and laser engraving.

4

Build partnerships & sponsorships

Find sponsors to support teacher salaries, materials and maintenance — making the program financially sustainable.
