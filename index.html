Who We Are

A small team with a big heart

After a private visit to the children's home with her family in the summer of 2024, Marcella L. had an idea: to open up space for creative thinking and hands-on making. Andreas S. and Anne S. later joined her — together we believe that creativity and practical skills can transform lives. In partnership with Hands for an African Child (HFAAC) in Bukomero, Uganda, we run hands-on crafting and skills programs first and foremost for the orphaned children of HFAAC — while their teachers and parents grow right alongside them, learning new skills of their own along the way.

Our first volunteers, Hannah and Emily, arrived in February 2025 and spent six weeks laying the groundwork. Since then, 15 volunteers have taught on-site for a combined ten weeks, and we've helped hire and train seven local trainers — three in computer skills, two in crocheting and handcrafts, one in machine sewing, and one in motorbike riding & maintenance — who now teach every week during term time. We're already back for our second intensive course in July 2026.

We are not a large NGO. We are people who show up, roll up their sleeves and craft something meaningful — together with the community.

Our Projects

From idea to reality

Each visit brings new workshops, new skills and new friendships. Here is how 2025 unfolded, week by week — and where we are now.

2025 Timeline
February – April 2025

Hannah & Emily — our first volunteers

The project kicks off! Over six weeks, Hannah and Emily work with the children on bracelets, carved figures, sewn pillows, clay, mobiles — and bake a lot of cookies together. They lay the groundwork for everything that follows.

May 2025

Teacher Esther starts teaching textiles

Our first local craft teacher joins. Esther specializes in traditional techniques like weaving baskets and mats from banana fibre, and begins regular textile lessons with the children.

July – August 2025

Teacher Filder joins — Boris, Sarah & Laura visit

Teacher Filder starts teaching textile work in July. In August, volunteers Boris, Sarah and Laura spend 2.5 weeks on-site, explore the local market, buy the first jigsaws and drills, and connect with Patrick, a foster father at HFAAC with a real talent for woodwork — expanding the program into technical crafts.

📅 31 October – 13 November 2025 · HFAAC Bukomero

Project 1 – Volunteer Skills Program

Marcella L., Andreas S. and Anne S. spend two weeks on-site leading the first dedicated project week — around 40 children work in depth on technical and textile skills. Teachers Esther and Filder help lead the handcrafting sessions, and Patrick, a foster father at HFAAC with a natural talent for woodwork, joins as our third local craft teacher. Every goal we set out with was achieved — and all three have kept the program running every week since we left in November 2025.

📅 4 – 19 July 2026 · HFAAC Bukomero

Project 2 – Building on What We Started

Back in Bukomero for our second intensive course! This visit we tackle two of our big 2026 goals at once: setting up a computer lab and introducing sewing machines — alongside advanced woodworking and crochet. Our local team grows to seven trainers: three in computer skills, one in machine sewing, one in motorbike riding & maintenance, and two continuing in crocheting and handcrafts. 🔜 Next Up
📅 Coming soon · HFAAC Bukomero

Project 3 – Connecting HFAAC

Our next project is already in the pipeline: bringing internet to the entire HFAAC compound — from the medical center and community building to the teacher homes, office buildings and all the way up to the family homes. We'll start with a microwave data link over a fibre (glasfibre) connection with WiFi access points across the site, and switch to Starlink once it becomes available in the area.

A huge thank you to Terry from the US, who is generously supporting this project — and who will help bring Starlink to HFAAC once it's available in the area.

Future Vision

Where we are going

With continued support, Crafting Futures aims to grow into a self-sustaining craft education centre that empowers generations of young people in Bukomero.

✓

Build a computer lab — achieved July 2026

One of our top 2026 goals, done: a fully equipped computer lab is now up and running at Nuri Primary School, teaching the children their first digital skills.

✓

Introduce sewing machines — achieved July 2026

Alongside hand sewing, students are now learning to use proper sewing machines and repair textiles — another 2026 goal reached during our second intensive course.

3

Multiple intensive courses every year

Grow from occasional visits to several dedicated project weeks per year with volunteers, so more children can go deeper on technical, textile and future computer skills.

4

Build partnerships & sponsorships

Good progress already: most of our local trainers have been added to HFAAC's official payroll, sponsored by HFAAC's Brian and Brad — a big step toward making the program financially sustainable for the long run.

Our Partner

Hands for an African Child

Crafting Futures operates in close partnership with Hands for an African Child (HFAAC) — a children's village for orphans in Bukomero, Uganda, built up since 2022. HFAAC provides the infrastructure, community connections and local support that make our work possible.

  • 12 families care for up to 16 orphaned children each, giving them a stable home with their basic needs covered
  • Its own school on the grounds — Nuri Primary School
  • A large, self-run farm that helps finance the village
  • Founded by Americans Brian Matthews and Brad Andersen, but locally led and run, financed through donations and agriculture
